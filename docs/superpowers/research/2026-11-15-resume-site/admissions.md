Labels: **Official** = a university, program, publisher, standards body, platform or employer's own page. **Expert** = named faculty, career centre, UX research firm, consultant (consultants flagged). **Anecdote** = one person's site or post.

### Common pattern (synthesis, n=14, self-selected, says nothing about admission odds)
- **Shape:** one page, 5–8 sections: intro/About → Education or Experience → Projects → Publications → Skills/Awards → Contact. Research-leaning owners put Education right after About (Vishal, Ashwin, Arystan); industry-leaning owners put Experience first and Education low (Rithik, Sushmit, Suresh, Sagar, Trent).
- **Grades:** 7/14 show a grade. Indian-degree owners who show one mostly show it as issued ("9.4/10", "9.29/10", "9.38/10"); three show a self-converted or re-expressed figure (3.85/4.0, 3.61/4.00, 90.5%). **No site shows a class rank with a cohort size.** Only one shows a named distinction (Director's Gold Medal, with what it was for).
- **Experience bullets:** action + tech + one number. Team size appears in 3 of ~28 quoted entries ("team of 4", "2-3 associates", "4 engineers"). Dates on projects in 2/14 sites.
- **CV file:** 9/14 link a CV (PDF or Google Drive). Two have broken or duplicate CV links.
- **Publications:** 8/14 list papers. Formats vary: only Vishal (full venue + month) and Daniyaal (all authors, preprint label, arXiv link) would satisfy Toronto's citation rule; none bolds the owner's name; one has a venue/DOI mismatch.
- **Writing/blog:** substantive only on 1/14 (Trent); 2 have empty or stale blog sections.
- **Visible errors:** wrong dates, dead links, empty sections, counters showing 0, a headline claim that the list beneath contradicts. These are the "half-done profile" risk the report warns about.
- **Discretion:** most owners are students and openly say "seeking roles" or name their program. None is a model for an employed applicant who must not signal.

## 2. Indian CGPA, rank and an unfamiliar university on a web page

- **Official (CMU SCS, Fall 2027 cycle, read 15 Nov 2026):** the SCS graduate application FAQ defines class rank as a "numerical representation of a student's academic standing within their graduating class", asks for it "as a number" with class size as a number, and says "Remember that these are optional fields." https://www.cs.cmu.edu/education/graduate-admissions (redirect from https://www.scs.cmu.edu/academics/application_instructions). The MSE is named in the fee/deadline section, so its applicants use this form. Implication: rank is read as rank-of-N; the page and the form should say the same thing.
- **Official (other schools, search snippets):** Stanford Biosciences FAQ: enter GPA as on the transcript, do not convert; "You are asked to enter both GPA and GPA scale" https://biosciences.stanford.edu/faqs/if-my-school-does-not-use-a-4-0-gpa-grading-scale-how-should-i-report-this-on-my-application/ . Yale Jackson: "Please do not convert or recalculate your GPA" https://jackson.yale.edu/faq/how-do-i-convert-my-gpa . Princeton SPIA: "Always use the scale provided by your institution" https://spia.princeton.edu/node/66376 . Counter-case: Columbia Business School asks for conversion with its own table (GMAT Club compilation, unverified) https://gmatclub.com/blog/28890/ .
- **Expert, consultant (GradPilot, Nirmal Thacker, 30 Oct 2026):** give "one calibratable number": a rank with its denominator ("4th of 118") or a cohort average printed on the transcript; "a missing number beats an invented one"; if no rank is published "do not estimate one"; a guessed figure contradicted by a recommender's form "does more harm than silence"; avoid prestige claims about the university and league-table positions; Stanford's recommender form asks for the comparison group, its size and percentile band. https://gradpilot.com/news/explain-strict-grading-unknown-university-sop
- **Anecdote (contrast):** Trent's "top ~5%" is an estimate he says he had to "deduce" because the school would not release figures (https://matthewtrent.me/blog/a/columbia-ms-cs-application). Fine on a blog; it is the kind of number GradPilot says not to state in an application.
- **Toronto SGS (already in report):** India maps by CGPA/"First Class"; no new official table found. Third-party benchmarks conflict (gradright, leapscholar); ignore them.
- **Synthesis for a page:** "CGPA 9.63 / 10" + "Rank 1 of [N], [branch], [graduating year]" + "SGPA 10.00 in semesters [a] and [b]" + any distinction exactly as printed on the degree or rank certificate. No 4.0 conversion, no percentage conversion, no NIRF/NAAC rank, no "top x%" unless a document states it.

## 3. Publications on a personal site

- **Official (Crossref display guidelines, 2017, current):** display DOIs as full HTTPS links (`https://doi.org/10.xxxx/...`); "doi:" prefix is superseded. https://www.crossref.org/display-guidelines (also https://doi.org/10.13003/5jchdy)
- **Official (NISO CRediT, ANSI/NISO Z39.104-2022):** 14 contributor roles (e.g., Conceptualization, Methodology, Software, Investigation, Writing – original draft, Supervision, Project administration) with optional "lead / equal / supporting" qualifiers. A standard vocabulary for a contribution line. https://niso.org/publications/z39104-2022-credit ; https://casrai.org/credit/
- **Official (IEEE author posting policy):** authors may post the accepted manuscript on a personal website with the IEEE copyright notice and a link/full citation to the published version; the publisher's final PDF is not for personal sites. https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/post-publication-policies/ ; conferences: https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/post-publication-policies/ ; https://editor-portal.ieee.org/publishing-workflow/after-article-acceptance/
- **Official (Springer Nature self-archiving):** rules differ by format; proceedings papers' accepted manuscript may go on the author's own site immediately on acceptance; subscription journal articles may have an embargo (6 or 12 months by portfolio; check the title). https://www.springernature.com/gp/open-research/policies/journal-policies ; https://support.nature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000257341-self-archiving
- **Official (UGC, India, 11 Feb 2025):** UGC stopped maintaining the UGC-CARE journal list, citing over-centralisation and continued inclusion of predatory journals; a static list "as on 10 Feb 2025" remains. So "UGC-CARE listed" is no longer a live quality badge, and was never one a US/Canadian reader knows. https://theprint.in/india/ugc-discontinues-care-journal-list-suggests-parameters-for-choosing-peer-reviewed-journals/2489617 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Grants_Commission_Consortium_for_Academic_and_Research_Ethics
- **Expert (career centres, search snippets):** UChicago Graduate career guides: "Follow the format used in your field. Bold your name in author list." https://grad.uchicago.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/CV-Best-Practices.pdf . Grinnell: list a co-authored manuscript by review stage ("submitted for review; under review; in revision") https://career.grinnell.edu/resources/publications/ . Philosophy-profession norm: unaccepted work under a separate heading, not "Publications" https://dailynous.com/?p=56614 . J-PAL: full citation with journal information and DOI on a personal site https://www.povertyactionlab.org/node/8136336 .
- **Observed on admit sites (Anecdote):** formats inconsistent; one venue/DOI mismatch (Rithik Sachdev); one model preprint label (Daniyaal Farooqi); none bold own name.
- **Synthesis:** Toronto's rule (all authors, venue, date, pages, peer-review status) + bold own name + venue exactly as on the proceedings/journal + type (conference/journal/workshop) + publisher + DOI as full URL + accepted-manuscript PDF only if the publisher allows + one contribution line in CRediT-style verbs.

## 4. Projects and the venture

- **Expert (NN/g, F-pattern, 2006, re-confirmed 2017):** readers scan the top and down the left; "start subheads, paragraphs, and bullet points with information-carrying words". https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/ ; https://www.nngroup.com/articles/text-scanning-patterns-eyetracking/
- **Observed (Anecdote, n=14):** project entries rarely carry role, team size or dates; the bar is low, so a one-line "what / my part / team / result / date / link" beats most admitted sites.
- **Official (Google Play Console Help):** for a personal developer account the "Legal name (shown on Google Play)", the country of the legal address, and a public contact email are displayed; organisation accounts show legal name, address, email and phone. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14177239?hl=en . Implication: the venture is already publicly tied to the owner's legal name (or company) on Play; the page must match it.
- **Install counts (Expert/anecdote; Google does not document thresholds):** Play shows a lower-bound range ("1K+" can mean 1,000–4,999). https://medium.com/cpidroid/how-google-displays-the-app-installs-count-on-google-play-listing-1bf59a21cbba ; https://www.androidenterprise.community/discussions/conversations/play-store-number-of-downloads-counter/11604 . Quote the listing's figure with a date; exact numbers come from Play Console.
- **Expert (startup-application writing, weak):** specificity, metrics, evidence; non-revenue traction (users, experiments) counts if labelled as such. https://www.every.io/blueprint-posts/how-to-write-a-killer-application-for-top-startup-incubators ; https://techcrunch.com/?p=2573433 . No graduate-admissions source on describing a side venture was found.
- **Official (employer codes, examples only):** Bank of England staff must "Seek pre-approval from your Head of Division" for outside roles https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/about/governance-and-funding/staff-codes-and-policies/other-employment ; other bank codes require prior written approval for outside employment/business (e.g., https://www.burgan.com/Doc/EmployeeCodeofConduct.pdf). Pattern: outside business activity needs pre-approval. The candidate's own bank's code governs; not checked.

## 5. Leadership and initiative evidence

- **Official (Stanford GSB, MBA, "How You Lead", undated, read 15 Nov 2026):** "We look for examples of when you have taken initiative, persisted through challenges, … engaged others in your efforts, and supported those around you." "You do not need to hold a specific role nor reach a certain level or title to show leadership." Activities go in the application sections and the résumé. https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/programs/mba/admission/evaluation-criteria . An MBA source; used as the clearest official statement of what "leadership" means to a professional-degree reader.
- **Official (Berkeley Fung FAQ, read 15 Nov 2026):** looks for a balance of technical and leadership skills; experienced professionals welcome to strengthen both; no résumé or website guidance. https://funginstitute.berkeley.edu/programs-centers/full-time-program/learn-more/application-faq/ . Fung style guide: "Industry experience will be an advantage in the leadership and Capstone courses." https://funginstitute.berkeley.edu/style-guide (snippet).
- **Official (Cornell Tech, from notes):** recommenders should discuss "the applicant's role in substantial projects". https://tech.cornell.edu/admissions/meng-application/
- **Observed:** admit sites show leadership as a titled section (Arystan "Leadership"; Rithik "team of 55"; Ronit "managing 60+ members"), i.e., scope numbers attached to roles.
- **Synthesis:** four behaviours (initiative, persistence, engaging others, developing others) each tied to one dated fact with a scope number and an outcome; titles optional.

## 6. Do readers click the site link, and what do they see first (only what the report lacks)

- **Official (CMU SCS application page, Fall 2027):** lists résumé contents ("education, research experience, work experience, publications, scholarships awarded") and a video URL; no website/GitHub/LinkedIn field mentioned. https://www.cs.cmu.edu/education/graduate-admissions
- **Official (Waterloo MDSAI co-op page, read 15 Nov 2026):** materials are résumé, three references ("at least two academic"), a statement of interest "up to 500 words", program-specific questions; minimum 78%; deadline January 15; no website/portfolio mention. https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/math/data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-co-op-master-data
- **Official (CMU undergraduate apply book, not graduate):** personal websites are among forms of interest CMU does "not consider" because they "can run counter to an accessible and inclusive admission process". https://www.cmu.edu/admission/sites/default/files/inline-files/CMU%202023%20Apply%20Book_0.pdf . Undergraduate only; signals an equity rationale for not reading outside links.
- **Expert (faculty, PhD/thesis context):** University of Calgary CS (Richard Zhao): the CV should start with name, contact and links to personal website/portfolio, LinkedIn or GitHub "if they are professionally maintained". https://cspages.ucalgary.ca/~richard.zhao1/prospective_notes.html . Columbia (Eugene Wu lab): "links to relevant and well documented github repositories will be helpful". https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~ewu/apply.html
- **Anecdote:** Trent submitted a live portfolio with every application (offer CMU MSE-SS and Columbia; reject Cornell Tech); he does not claim it was read. https://matthewtrent.me/blog/a/columbia-ms-cs-application
- **Expert (NN/g, Therese Fessenden, 15 Apr 2018):** "users spent about 57% of their page-viewing time above the fold"; "74% of the viewing time was spent in the first two screenfuls"; "Reserve the top of the page for high-priority content". General web users, not admissions readers. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/
- **No source found** that reports what an admissions reader looks at first on a personal site. UofT DCS ambassador guide (Helen Li, 20 Mar 2025) covers résumés only. https://www.teach.cs.toronto.edu/ambassadors/guides/masters-app/
